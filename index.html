YOUR PHONE. YOUR BOUNDARIES.

Data control shouldn’t mean giving up privacy.

Nibble needs Android’s system network-control permission to see app-level data use and apply the controls you choose. The work happens on your device—not by sending your traffic through our servers.

Private by design. Messages, browsing history, and passwords are never collected.

What it uses

Android’s network-control permission to understand data use by app and apply your choices.

What stays yours

Your messages, browsing history, and passwords. They’re not part of this.

No traffic detour

Your connection isn’t routed through our servers to see which apps are using data.

Android may show a system indicator when network-control access is active. You stay in charge of your permissions.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you join.

Here’s what you might be wondering.

Does Nibble work on iPhone?

We’re starting with Android. iPhone support isn’t available right now.

Will Nibble be free?

Pricing hasn’t been announced yet. Join the waitlist and we’ll share the details as they’re ready.

Does the app use my mobile data too?

Nibble is designed to stay lightweight. The usage view on this page is an illustrative preview of the product.

Which mobile networks will it work with?

It’s designed for Android mobile data, not a single carrier. MTN, Airtel, and Glo are examples shown here—not a list of confirmed launch partners.

Why does it need a network permission?

Android requires network-control access for app-level usage visibility and controls. Your data traffic doesn’t need to pass through our servers.
